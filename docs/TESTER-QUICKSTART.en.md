# Lean Ritual — 2-Minute Tester Quick-Start

**Lean Ritual** (`薄肌日课`) is an invite-only web app for logging daily workouts, meals, and weight at `https://leanritual.app`—runs right in your browser.

## Get started

- **Open the link:** Use your invite link or visit `https://leanritual.app` (turn off VPN if testing from Mainland China).
- **Sign up:** Under **Sign up with invite**, use your invited email and an 8+ character password, tap **Sign up & send verification email**, verify (check spam/junk), sign in, and tap **Build my plan**.
- **Add to home screen:** iPhone Safari **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**; Android browser menu `⋮` → **Add to Home screen** or **Install app**.

## Try these

- **Today — Daily Check-in:** Enter weight, steps, or sleep, then tap **Save check-in**.
- **Meals — Meal photo + AI estimate:** Attach a food photo, choose **AI estimate**, and tap **Save & estimate with AI** (or **Manual**).
- **Today / Log — Log a workout set:** Enter weight and reps for a set, tap **Finish & save workout**, and check **Log**.
- **Feedback — AI feedback:** Under **Ask AI Coach**, tap **Analyze with AI**, tap **Adopt** (**Today only** / **Long-term** / **Decline**), then try **Applied · Undo** (**Undo**).
- **Me — Language switch:** Tap **中文 / English** at the top right.
- **Me — Export / delete account:** Under **Account**, tap **Export my data & photos**, or expand **Delete account** → **Delete account permanently**.

## Report a problem

Found a bug or idea? Tap **Me → Send feedback** right inside the app (under 30 seconds, optional 1 screenshot, automatically attaches version & device info without health data). If you can't sign in, tap **Can't sign in? Email feedback** at the bottom of the login page (never send passwords or URL tokens).
