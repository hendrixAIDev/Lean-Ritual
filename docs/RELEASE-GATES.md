## 5. Known Risks Accepted for Beta

- **No offsite backup (7-day server-only retention):** Daily and pre-deploy snapshots + deletion ledgers reside on the same Aliyun host (`BACKUP_RETENTION_DAYS=7`, keeping at least the 2 newest valid snapshots per type and latest deletion ledger; `OFFSITE_DEST` disabled by owner decision). Whole-host or disk loss loses server data unless Aliyun cloud disk snapshots or an offsite SSH target are later enabled.
- **Single-server brief maintenance pauses:** Deployments and daily backups stop `bojin.service` briefly under the maintenance lock for consistent offline snapshots; Caddy's 15 s retry window + `shim.js` read/write retries mask short restarts, while in-flight photo/AI POSTs require user retry if interrupted.
- **US West cross-border routing without CDN/ICP:** Traffic goes directly to Aliyun Silicon Valley (`47.254.72.103`, Cloudflare DNS-only); mainland evening-peak latency/jitter is subject to carrier international egress.
- **Request-count AI quotas (not USD caps) in app:** `AI_DAILY_LIMIT` (`60`/user/day), `AI_GLOBAL_DAILY_LIMIT` (`300`/day), and `AI_MAX_OUTPUT_TOKENS` (`2000`) bound volume, so dollar exposure depends on the OpenRouter API key's configured monthly spend cap.
- **Append-only history & fixed login email:** `profileHistory` and `workoutLog` snapshots persist under the user's 50 MiB / 20k-doc quota until account deletion, and registration email cannot be self-changed in-app (disclosed in `public/privacy.html`).
- **Offline multi-device `localStorage` residue:** Logout and account deletion clear `bj-user:<uid>:*` storage in the current browser, but cannot remotely wipe `localStorage` on separate offline devices (`public/privacy.html` advises private browsing on shared devices).
- **English Better Auth error strings on `/login.html`:** Server-side Better Auth rejections (e.g., `"Invalid email or password"`, `"Email not verified"`) currently display in English rather than Chinese.
