薄肌训练与饮食纲领（BLS 理论版）

总纲

目标是在保留并增加肌肉的前提下，把体脂降到 10% 左右并长期维持在 10–13% 区间。“薄肌”不是特殊的肌肉，而是“足够的肌肉量 + 低体脂”。

本纲领以 Michael Matthews《Bigger, Leaner, Stronger》（以下简称 BLS）的方法为理论底座，以你的“薄肌战神”计划为执行框架。书中内容为我根据对该书的了解所做的概括，并非原文摘录。BLS 的核心可以归成六条：

  1. 渐进式超负荷是增肌的第一驱动力：用大重量复合动作，力量持续上涨，肌肉才会持续增长。泵感和酸痛不是目标。

  2. 热量决定体重方向，蛋白质决定掉的是脂肪还是肌肉：减脂靠热量缺口，增肌靠小幅盈余，任何阶段蛋白质都要足。

  3. 宏量营养素要算，但食物选择可以灵活：大部分热量来自营养密度高的天然食物，少量自由度换长期坚持。

  4. 有氧是辅助工具：少量、低冲击，不能挤占力量训练的恢复。

  5. 补剂只是锦上添花：只有少数几种有扎实证据，训练和饮食没到位时补剂无意义。

  6. 周期化：减脂、维持、精益增肌按体脂阈值切换；训练每隔一段时间减载，让身体恢复。

训练

每周 3 练，推 / 拉 / 腿+肩，复合动作 4–6 次大重量，孤立动作 8–15 次，按“双重渐进”加重。这与 BLS 的 3 天版本结构基本一致，只在第 3 天补了一个腿后侧动作。

加重规则（双重渐进）

  1. 在同一重量下不断加次数，直到第一个正式组做到区间上限（复合动作就是 6 次）。

  2. 下一次训练加重：下肢和硬拉 +5 kg，卧推、推举、划船 +2.5 kg，哑铃每只 +1–2 kg。

  3. 加重后如果第一组不到 4 次，退回原重量再多打磨一两次。

  4. 大部分正式组停在力竭前 1–2 次，动作变形就停。硬拉和深蹲不做到力竭。

  5. 每次记录重量×次数，连续 3 次训练一个动作没有进步，先查睡眠和热量，再考虑换动作变体。

减脂期力量能保持不退就是成功，不必强求每次都涨。

热身与减载

  • 热身组（每天第一个复合动作）：正式重量的 50% × 10、70% × 4、90% × 1，组间休息 1 分钟左右。后续复合动作做 1–2 个轻组即可。

  • 减载：每连续训练 8–10 周，安排 1 周减载（重量降到约 60%，组数减半）或完全休息。关节疼、力量连续下滑、睡不好时可提前。

  • 无器械备用：俯卧撑、引体向上、深蹲（可单腿或背负重），同样按加次数→加负重渐进。

饮食

先算 TDEE（每日总消耗），再按阶段定热量，先定蛋白质和脂肪，剩下的给碳水。公式只是起点，最终以两周体重趋势校准。

第 1 步：基础代谢 BMR

知道体脂率时用 Katch-McArdle（BLS 也基于瘦体重思路）：

\text{BMR} = 370 + 21.6 \times \text{LBM}_{kg}, \quad \text{LBM} = \text{体重} \times (1 - \text{体脂率})

不知道体脂率时用 Mifflin-St Jeor（男性）：

\text{BMR} = 10 \times \text{体重}_{kg} + 6.25 \times \text{身高}_{cm} - 5 \times \text{年龄} + 5

第 2 步：TDEE 与阶段热量

\text{TDEE} = \text{BMR} \times \text{活动系数}

活动系数起步值：每周 3 练 + 日均 5000 步以下取 1.35；每周 3 练 + 日均约 10000 步取 1.45–1.5。

你的“体重几斤就吃几克蛋白质”等于 2 g/kg，和 BLS 减脂期的建议几乎一致，可以直接当日常口诀。体脂高于 20% 时，改按目标体重或 2.5 g/kg LBM 计算，避免蛋白质溢出。

第 3 步：碳水拿剩余

\text{碳水}_{g} = \frac{\text{目标热量} - 4 \times \text{蛋白质}_{g} - 9 \times \text{脂肪}_{g}}{4}

示例（假设数据，填好个人参数后替换）：65 kg、15% 体脂、日均 1 万步。LBM ≈ 55 kg，BMR ≈ 1560 kcal，TDEE ≈ 2260 kcal，减脂目标 ≈ 1770 kcal：蛋白质 143 g、脂肪 45 g、碳水约 197 g。

校准规则

  • 每天晨起空腹称重，只看 7 日均值。

  • 减脂期连续 2 周周均下降不到 0.5%：每日减 100–150 kcal（从碳水或脂肪扣），或日均多走 2000 步。

  • 下降超过每周 1%、力量明显下滑：加回 100–150 kcal。

  • 增肌期每月涨幅超过 1%：减 100 kcal。

食物规则

  • 蛋白质分 3–4 顿吃，每顿 30–50 g；训练前后 2 小时内各有一顿含蛋白和碳水。

  • 热量的 80–90% 来自天然食物：米饭、薯类、燕麦、水果、蔬菜、鸡蛋、肉鱼、奶制品。

  • 默认不吃添加糖饮料和零食，奶茶可乐排在首位。偶尔在宏量额度内吃一点不算破戒（取舍见最后一节）。

  • 有氧：默认日均 8000–10000 步。减脂后期需要加大缺口时，再加每周 2 次 20 分钟单车或划船间歇训练，每周有氧总时长不超过力量训练时长的一半。

  • 睡眠 7–9 小时，是减脂期保肌肉最被低估的因素。

补剂

只保留证据最扎实的三样：蛋白粉、肌酸、咖啡因。其余按需补缺，不买燃脂类产品。

阶段按体脂率切换：高于 15% 先减脂，到 10% 左右转维持，再视需要进入精益增肌，涨到 13–15% 再减回来。训练每 8–10 周一个大周期，以减载周收尾。

Lean Ritual Training & Nutrition Guide (BLS Edition)

Overview

The goal is to preserve and build muscle while bringing body fat down to around 10% and maintaining it sustainably in the 10–13% range. An athletic "lean" physique is not a special kind of muscle — it is simply enough muscle mass paired with low body fat.

This guide uses the methodology of Michael Matthews's Bigger, Leaner, Stronger (BLS) as its theoretical foundation and the Lean Ritual program as its execution framework. References to the book are our own summary of its concepts rather than verbatim excerpts. BLS distills into six core principles:

  1. Progressive overload is the primary driver of muscle growth: lift heavy on compound movements and steadily build strength over time so muscle continues to grow. Chasing a pump or soreness is not the goal.

  2. Calories dictate weight direction; protein dictates whether you lose fat or muscle: fat loss requires a calorie deficit, muscle gain requires a modest surplus, and protein stays high in every phase.

  3. Track macros while keeping food choices flexible: get most calories from nutrient-dense whole foods, leaving a small margin of flexibility for long-term adherence.

  4. Cardio is a supporting tool: keep it modest and low-impact so it never cuts into recovery from strength training.

  5. Supplements are minor extras: only a handful have solid evidence, and none matter unless training and nutrition are dialed in.

  6. Periodization: switch between Cut, Maintain, and Lean Bulk phases by body-fat thresholds, and schedule periodic deload weeks so joints and the nervous system recover.

Training

Train 3 days per week on a Push / Pull / Legs + Shoulders split: heavy compound lifts for 4–6 reps and isolation movements for 8–15 reps, progressing via double progression. This mirrors the 3-day BLS structure with an added posterior-chain leg movement on Day 3.

Progression Rules (Double Progression)

  1. Add reps at the same working weight until your first working set hits the top of the prescribed rep range (6 reps on compounds).

  2. Increase load next session: +5 kg on lower-body lifts and deadlifts, +2.5 kg on bench/press/row, and +1–2 kg per dumbbell.

  3. If set 1 drops below 4 reps after moving up, return to the previous weight and consolidate for another session or two.

  4. End most working sets 1–2 reps shy of failure and stop as soon as form breaks. Never take squats or deadlifts to failure.

  5. Log weight × reps every workout; if an exercise stalls for 3 consecutive sessions, check sleep and calories first before swapping to a variation.

During a cut, holding onto your strength is a win — you do not need to force weight increases every week.

Warm-Up & Deloads

  • Warm-up sets (first compound lift of the day): 50% × 10, 70% × 4, and 90% × 1 of working weight, resting ~1 minute between warm-up sets. Follow-up compounds need only 1–2 light ramp-up sets.

  • Deload: every 8–10 weeks of continuous training, take 1 deload week (~60% working weight and half the sets) or full rest. Deload early if joints ache, strength drops across multiple sessions, or sleep suffers.

  • No-equipment fallback: push-ups, pull-ups, and bodyweight/single-leg/backpack squats using the same reps-then-load progression.

Nutrition

Estimate TDEE (total daily energy expenditure) first, set calories for your current phase, lock in protein and fat, and allocate the remaining calories to carbs. Formulas are only a starting point — calibrate from your 2-week weight trend.

Step 1: Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR)

When body fat % is known, use Katch-McArdle (aligned with BLS's lean-mass approach):

\text{BMR} = 370 + 21.6 \times \text{LBM}_{kg}, \quad \text{LBM} = \text{Weight} \times (1 - \text{BodyFat})

When body fat % is unknown, use Mifflin-St Jeor (male):

\text{BMR} = 10 \times \text{Weight}_{kg} + 6.25 \times \text{Height}_{cm} - 5 \times \text{Age} + 5

Step 2: TDEE & Phase Calories

\text{TDEE} = \text{BMR} \times \text{ActivityFactor}

Starting activity multipliers: 1.35 for 3 workouts/week + under 5,000 daily steps; 1.45–1.5 for 3 workouts/week + ~10,000 daily steps.

A simple rule of thumb of ~2 g/kg (~0.9–1 g/lb) closely matches the BLS cutting recommendation. When body fat exceeds 20%, calculate protein from goal weight or 2.5 g/kg LBM instead so protein isn't overestimated.

Step 3: Carbs Take the Remainder

\text{Carbs}_{g} = \frac{\text{TargetCalories} - 4 \times \text{Protein}_{g} - 9 \times \text{Fat}_{g}}{4}

Example (illustrative numbers): 65 kg, 15% body fat, 10,000 daily steps. LBM ≈ 55 kg, BMR ≈ 1,560 kcal, TDEE ≈ 2,260 kcal, Cut target ≈ 1,770 kcal: 143 g protein, 45 g fat, ~197 g carbs.

Calibration Rules

  • Weigh yourself every morning fasted after using the bathroom, and evaluate only the 7-day average.

  • Cutting and losing less than 0.5%/week for 2 straight weeks: reduce daily intake by 100–150 kcal (from carbs or fat) or add 2,000 daily steps.

  • Losing faster than 1%/week with noticeable strength loss: add back 100–150 kcal/day.

  • Lean bulking and gaining faster than 1%/month: trim 100 kcal/day.

Food & Lifestyle Rules

  • Split protein across 3–4 meals (30–50 g per meal), including a meal with protein and carbs within 2 hours before and after lifting.

  • Get 80–90% of calories from minimally processed whole foods: rice, potatoes, oats, fruit, vegetables, eggs, meat, fish, and dairy.

  • Skip sugary drinks and added-sugar snacks by default (sodas and sweetened teas first). Occasional small treats fitted inside your daily macros do not ruin progress.

  • Cardio: default to 8,000–10,000 daily steps. Late in a cut if needed, add two 20-minute low-impact cycling or rowing interval sessions per week, keeping weekly cardio under half your total lifting time.

  • Sleep 7–9 hours per night — the most underrated factor in preserving muscle during a cut.

Supplements

Stick to the three supplements backed by the strongest evidence: whey protein, creatine, and caffeine. Add micronutrients only to cover gaps, and skip commercial "fat burners."

Periodization & Tracking

Switch phases by body fat %: cut first when above 15% until reaching ~10%, stabilize at maintenance, then lean bulk if desired until 13–15% before cutting again. Run training in 8–10 week mesocycles ending with a deload week.

Start every phase from the formula estimate, run it consistently for 2 weeks, and adjust using the calibration rules.
