我们保存什么

账号与安全方面，我们保存你提供的邮箱、称呼、加密哈希形式的密码、受邀注册记录，以及登录会话和邮箱验证、密码重置凭证。IP 地址用于登录、注册、密码重置及错误上报等请求的频率限制，并随登录会话记录；登录会话与应用诊断信息中还会记录浏览器标识（User-Agent）。当登录页或应用发生运行错误时，应用还会向本服务器发送最小化的错误报告（仅含错误类型、预设错误提示、代码位置、页面名称、应用版本、语言、浏览器与系统大类、系统主版本及时间戳，不含个人数据、账号标识或记录内容），仅用于排查修复故障，不写入数据库，仅保留在服务器系统日志中（按 journald 保留期自动清理）。照片上传、AI 调用与应用内反馈均按账号与全局限额。

健康与训练记录保存在你自己的账号下，包括：身体指标、训练计划偏好、伤病与补剂说明；每日打卡（体重、步数、睡眠、体脂、腰围等）、训练记录及自动同步的训练草稿（含重量次数、体感、疼痛与备注）；饮食记录、营养估算、AI 反馈及收藏的动作链接。每次修改资料前的旧资料快照与每次完成训练另存的训练备份快照也会保存在账号下，直至删除账号。上传的照片会重新编码并去除 EXIF 等原始元数据（若上传失败，餐食记录中可能保留本地重绘的压缩缩略图）。我们不需要你的 Claude 账号、订阅或个人 AI API 密钥。

当你在「我的」→「意见反馈」主动提交应用内意见反馈时，我们会在你的账号下保存反馈类型、反馈文字、你选择上传的 1 张截图（同样经重新编码并去除 EXIF 等元数据，适用相同的照片大小与配额限制）以及提交时间；若你保持勾选「附带设备信息」，还会一并保存应用版本、当前页面、标准化后的浏览器与系统版本、屏幕尺寸、界面语言、在线状态与客户端时间，但不会自动附带你的健康记录、餐食照片或个人资料。为及时响应测试问题，服务器还可能向运营者配置的告警通道（如 ntfy）发送一条不含正文的提醒，仅包含反馈类型、反馈编号、提交时间以及是否附带截图，不含反馈正文、账号标识、邮箱、称呼、截图或设备信息。登录页的邮件反馈链接由你自行通过邮箱应用发送。

数据在哪里处理

应用部署于阿里云美国西部（硅谷）服务器，在线数据存储于中国大陆以外；异地备份位置会在公测前列明。网页没有广告或分析跟踪器，不加载外部第三方脚本；登录使用必要的会话 Cookie，包装营养成分表的文字识别（OCR）也在浏览器本地完成。

填写训练组数、体感或备注时，停手约 2 秒会自动将训练草稿同步到你的在线账号（下次打开应用时也会尝试补传未同步的本地训练草稿）。浏览器本地存储（localStorage）还会按账号保留训练草稿、训练本地备份及未提交的饮食或反馈文字。退出登录时会先尝试同步未同步的训练草稿，再清除本设备浏览器中该账号的本地草稿与训练备份；在共享设备上仍请使用无痕/隐私窗口，或在使用后清除本站点的浏览器数据，并不要保存密码。

账号邮件

注册验证、重发验证邮件和重置密码时，系统通过 Resend 发送邮件，邮件服务会处理收件邮箱、一次性验证或重置链接及必要的投递信息。邮件服务商的处理地区与保留规则适用其各自政策，具体投递与保留细节将在上线前核实补充。请勿转发验证或密码重置链接。

使用 AI 时

AI 功能目前为可选项，服务器可能未开启 AI；你也可以随时手动记录而不调用 AI。当 AI 开启且你主动请求餐食估算、动作替换建议或周期反馈时，服务器会通过 OpenRouter 转发请求至服务端配置的模型供应商（开启时默认为 Google Gemini）。

视所选功能而定，发送内容可能包含：餐食文字描述、最多 4 张压缩照片（或本地识别出的包装文字）及当日营养目标；课表动作、场地经验、伤病说明与换动作原因；或个人资料摘要、反馈文字及最近 14 天的打卡、训练和饮食摘要，并附带应用名称及一个不含邮箱和称呼的匿名账号标识。OpenRouter 与底层模型供应商的处理地区及保留规则适用其各自政策，将在上线前补充，且可能与本应用不同。AI 估算和建议可能有误，你可以随时修改结果。

导出和删除

在「我的」账号区可导出自己的记录和照片。导出文件为 NDJSON（每行一个 JSON 对象），包含账号基本信息、全部在线文档（含资料变更历史与训练备份快照）、已提交的应用内反馈以及 Base64 编码的照片（含反馈截图），不含密码哈希或仅存于本机浏览器的未同步输入。文件含私人信息，请妥善保管。

删除账号需要输入密码并确认。删除后会撤销登录，清除服务器上的账号、认证与邀请记录、全部在线记录与历史快照、应用内反馈、照片（含反馈截图）及用量与限流统计，并清除当前浏览器中该账号的本地草稿与备份；曾在其他设备浏览器留下的本地草稿或已下载的导出文件不受影响，请在对应设备自行清理。中断的在线清理会自动重试。为避免恢复旧备份时找回已删账号，删除清单仅保留随机账号标识和删除时间，不保留邮箱、密码、健康记录、反馈内容或照片。

备份与保留

账号存续期间，在线记录与应用内反馈（含附带截图）会保留在账号下；其中每次改资料生成的旧资料快照、每次完成训练的备份快照以及应用内反馈不会随日常修改清除，会保留至删除账号为止。服务器本机保留约 7 天的每日备份和发布前快照（每类至少保留最新两份，不受天数限制，用于故障回退），暂无异地备份（整机故障时可能无法恢复数据）；删除账号后，超期且不在上述最低保留范围内的旧备份会自动清除，恢复旧备份时会重新应用最新删除清单。邮件、AI 与告警通知服务商已接收的数据适用其各自政策。

What We Store

For account and security purposes, we store the email address and display name you provide, your password as a cryptographic hash, your invited registration record, sign-in sessions, and email-verification and password-reset credentials. IP addresses are used to rate-limit authentication requests (such as sign-in, sign-up, and password reset) and error reports, and are recorded alongside sign-in sessions; browser identifiers (User-Agent) are also recorded in sign-in sessions and application diagnostic metadata. When a runtime error occurs on the login page or in the app, the client may also send minimal error reports to our server (containing only the error type, fixed diagnostic message, location in code, page name, app version, language, browser/OS family, OS major version, and timestamp, without personal data, user identifiers, or record contents), used solely to diagnose and fix bugs and kept in server logs only (subject to journald retention, never stored in the database). Photo uploads, AI requests, and in-app feedback are rate- and quota-limited per account and globally.

Health and training records are stored under your own account, including: body metrics, training plan preferences, and injury and supplement notes; daily check-ins (weight, steps, sleep, body fat, waist, etc.), workout logs, and auto-synced workout drafts (including weights, reps, session feel, pain, and notes); meal logs, nutrition estimates, AI feedback, and saved exercise video links. Pre-edit profile snapshots created whenever you modify your profile and workout backup snapshots saved whenever you finish a workout are also retained under your account until account deletion. Uploaded photos are re-encoded with EXIF and other original metadata stripped (if an upload fails, a locally redrawn compressed thumbnail may be kept in the meal entry). We do not require a Claude account, subscription, or personal AI API key.

When you submit in-app feedback from "Me" → "Send feedback", we store under your account the feedback type, your message, any single screenshot you choose to attach (re-encoded with EXIF and other metadata stripped under the same photo size and quota limits), and the submission timestamp. If you leave "Include device info" checked, we also store the app version, current tab/page, normalized browser/OS version, screen dimensions, interface language, online status, and client timestamp; we never automatically attach your health records, meal photos, or profile. To notify the operator promptly, the server may send a metadata-only alert to the operator's configured alert channel (such as ntfy) containing only the feedback type, feedback ID, submission timestamp, and whether a screenshot is attached — never your message text, account ID, email, display name, screenshot, or device info. The email feedback link on the sign-in page opens your own email client.

Where Data Is Processed

The application is deployed on Alibaba Cloud US West (Silicon Valley) servers, with online data stored outside mainland China; off-site backup locations will be specified before public beta. The web app contains no advertising or analytics trackers and loads no external third-party scripts; sign-in uses strictly necessary session cookies, and optical character recognition (OCR) for packaged nutrition labels runs locally in your browser.

When entering workout sets, session feel, or notes, pausing for about 2 seconds automatically syncs your workout draft to your online account (unsynced local workout drafts are also retried the next time you open the app). Browser local storage (localStorage) also keeps per-account workout drafts, local workout backups, and unsubmitted meal or feedback text. Signing out first attempts to sync any unsynced workout drafts and then clears that account's local drafts and workout backups in this device's browser; on shared devices, please still use an incognito/private window or clear site data for this origin after use, and do not save your password.

Account Emails

When verifying registration, resending verification emails, or resetting passwords, the system sends emails via Resend, which processes the recipient email address, one-time verification or reset links, and necessary delivery metadata. The email provider's processing regions and retention rules are governed by its own policies, and specific delivery and retention details will be verified and added before launch. Do not forward verification or password-reset links.

When Using AI

AI features are currently optional, and AI may not be enabled on the server; you can always log manually at any time without invoking AI. When AI is enabled and you actively request a meal estimate, exercise replacement suggestion, or periodic feedback, the server forwards the request through OpenRouter to the server-configured model provider (Google Gemini by default when enabled).

Depending on the selected feature, the transmitted content may include: meal text descriptions, up to 4 compressed photos (or locally extracted package text), and that day's nutrition targets; planned exercises, equipment and experience level, injury notes, and reason for swapping; or a profile summary, feedback text, and a summary of the last 14 days of check-ins, workouts, and meals, together with the application name and an anonymized account identifier that contains neither your email nor your display name. Processing regions and retention rules of OpenRouter and the underlying model provider are governed by their respective policies, will be added before launch, and may differ from this application's. AI estimates and suggestions may contain errors, and you can edit the results at any time.

Export and Deletion

In the Account section of the "Me" tab, you can export your own records and photos. The exported file is in NDJSON format (one JSON object per line) and contains basic account details, all online documents (including profile change history and workout backup snapshots), submitted in-app feedback, and Base64-encoded photos (including feedback screenshots); it excludes password hashes and unsynced inputs stored only in this device's browser. Because the file contains personal information, please store it securely.

Deleting your account requires entering your password and confirming. Deletion revokes sign-in sessions, removes your account, authentication and invitation records, all online records and historical snapshots, submitted in-app feedback, photos (including feedback screenshots), and usage/rate-limit counters from the server, and clears that account's local drafts and backups in the current browser; local drafts left in browsers on other devices or previously downloaded export files are unaffected — please clear them on those devices directly. Interrupted online cleanup is retried automatically. To prevent deleted accounts from being restored if an older backup is replayed, the deletion ledger retains only a random account identifier and deletion timestamp, without keeping your email, password, health records, feedback, or photos.

Backups and Retention

While your account remains active, online records and submitted in-app feedback (including attached screenshots) are kept under your account; pre-edit profile snapshots generated whenever you modify your profile, backup snapshots saved whenever you finish a workout, and in-app feedback are not removed by day-to-day edits and remain until account deletion. The server host retains approximately 7 days of local daily backups and pre-release snapshots (keeping at least the two newest of each category regardless of age for rollback recovery), with no off-site backups currently configured (data may not be recoverable in the event of a full host failure); after account deletion, older backups past their retention window and outside the minimum rollback set above are pruned automatically, and restoring an older backup reapplies the latest deletion ledger. Data already received by email, AI, and alert notification providers is governed by their respective policies.
