Depending on the selected feature, the transmitted content may include: meal text descriptions, up to 4 compressed photos (or locally extracted package text), and that day's nutrition targets; planned exercises, equipment and experience level, injury notes, and reason for swapping; or a profile summary, feedback text, and a summary of the last 14 days of check-ins, workouts, and meals. The server does not attach your email address, phone number, display name, or raw account ID in request metadata (requests forwarded via OpenRouter include only the application name and an HMAC-derived pseudonymous user identifier in metadata, while direct Google Gemini API requests omit any user metadata field). However, free-text notes and photos you submit are transmitted as supplied and may contain a name, email address, phone number, or other identifying details if you include them — please avoid putting unnecessary personal identifiers in meal notes, feedback text, or photos. AI estimates and suggestions may contain errors, are not medical advice, and can be edited at any time.

Export and Deletion

In the Account section of the "Me" tab, you can export your own records and photos at any time (even if you decline updated terms, you can still export your data or delete your account). The exported file is in NDJSON format (one JSON object per line) and contains basic account details (including your registered email address or phone number) and your consent version and timestamp, all online documents (including profile change history and workout backup snapshots), submitted in-app feedback, and Base64-encoded photos (including feedback screenshots); it excludes password hashes and unsynced inputs stored only in this device's browser. Because the file contains personal information, please store it securely.

Deleting your account requires entering your password and confirming. Deletion revokes sign-in sessions, removes your account, authentication, consent, and invitation records, all online records and historical snapshots, submitted in-app feedback, photos (including feedback screenshots), and usage/rate-limit counters from the server, and clears that account's local drafts and backups in the current browser; local drafts left in browsers on other devices or previously downloaded export files are unaffected — please clear them on those devices directly. Interrupted online cleanup is retried automatically. To prevent deleted accounts from being restored if an older backup is replayed, the deletion ledger retains only a random account identifier and deletion timestamp, without keeping your email, phone number, password, health records, feedback, or photos.

Backups and Retention

While your account remains active, online records and submitted in-app feedback (including attached screenshots) are kept under your account; pre-edit profile snapshots generated whenever you modify your profile, backup snapshots saved whenever you finish a workout, and in-app feedback are not removed by day-to-day edits and remain until account deletion. Invite requests submitted on the landing page are retained in the host database (and standard backups below) while managing beta invitations and preventing duplicate outreach; you may email hendrix.ai.dev@gmail.com at any time to request correction or deletion of an invite request. Under the application's configured backup retention policy, the server host retains approximately 7 days of local daily application backups and pre-release snapshots (keeping at least the two newest of each category regardless of age for rollback recovery); when Alibaba Cloud US West (Silicon Valley) same-region daily system-disk snapshots are enabled by the operator, the policy retains the 2 newest automatic snapshots (alongside 1 manual snapshot); no cross-region off-site backup is configured (actual backup and snapshot retention depends on live host configuration). After account deletion, older backups past their retention window and outside the minimum rollback set above are pruned automatically, and restoring an older backup reapplies the latest deletion ledger. Client error reports stay in server logs only and rotate automatically under journald retention. Data already received by email, AI, and alert notification providers is governed by their respective policies.
